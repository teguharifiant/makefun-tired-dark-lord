A 100-word villain monologue for the [make.fun bounty "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb).

A dark lord who has kept his tower, his Ember Crown and his whole empire of dread for three hundred years greets the hero who has finally come to duel him — and would really rather get on with his schedule. The tiredness lives in the pauses, the kettle, the complaining stairs and a left knee that files its grievances in triplicate; the villainy stays in the scheme, the history and the sword.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts            # writes entry.txt
node main.ts entry.txt  # same, explicit path
```

`main.ts` uses only the Node 22 standard library and writes the same bytes on every run.

## Entry

> Ah. The hero. Right on time, the one appointment I could not move.
>
> Sit. No, not there. That chair belongs to the cat, and the cat outranks you.
>
> Three hundred years I have kept this tower, the Ember Crown, the whole empire of dread, and still the kettle whistles at dawn, still the stairs complain, still my left knee files its grievances in triplicate.
>
> You want a duel? I have a monologue, a curse, and a schedule, and only one of them can be honoured.
>
> Draw your sword. But slowly. I have waited three centuries for someone to listen properly.

Exactly 100 words.
